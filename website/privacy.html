Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

wowMD is designed as a local-first Chrome extension for reading public GitHub Markdown pages.

Plain summary

wowMD does not collect personal information, upload documents, sell data, or track browsing history.

Controller

For privacy questions about wowMD, contact the maintainer through the feedback form linked on this website.

Data we process

The extension reads page content locally in your browser only when you choose Better View. It does not collect account data, cookies, browsing history, local files, or private repository content.

Purpose and legal basis

Processing is limited to providing the reading features you request, such as outline navigation, section folding, code rendering, and table layout. Where GDPR applies, this is based on providing the service you request and legitimate interest in operating a privacy-preserving browser tool.

Storage and retention

wowMD does not maintain a remote database for your reading activity. Any temporary processing happens in the browser session and is not sent to a wowMD server.

Sharing and third parties

wowMD does not sell or share reading data. If you submit feedback through the website, the information you choose to provide, such as your message, feature preferences, and optional email address, is used only to read feedback and follow up on requested features.

Chrome Web Store and Limited Use

wowMD uses browser permissions only to provide the disclosed reading features. Information received from Google APIs, if any, will be used in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

Depending on your location, including the European Economic Area, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port personal data. Because wowMD does not store reading data on a server, most requests can be answered by explaining what is and is not collected.

Children

wowMD is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If wowMD changes its data practices, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
